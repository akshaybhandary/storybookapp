## Variant: Warm Keepsake (premium editorial)

### Design stance
Position LumiFables as a Wonderbly-grade keepsake, not an AI toy — quiet luxury that justifies $39+ pricing.

### Key choices
- Layout: split hero with interactive before/after slider (photo → illustrated page) as THE proof
- Typography: Fraunces serif display + Fredoka body; warm cream paper background
- Color: terracotta #E67E50 on cream #FFF9F2, deep plum text
- Interaction: drag-to-compare slider; gift-card demo

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: gift-buyer trust, premium price anchoring, brand longevity
- Weak at: speed-to-action (creation is a click away, not in the hero)

### Best for
Grandparents, gift-givers, buyers who choose Wonderbly today.
