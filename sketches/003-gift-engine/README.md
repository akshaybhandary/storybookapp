## Variant: Gift Engine (occasion-driven conversion machine)

### Design stance
Sell the occasion, not the product — 52% of this market is gifting and 40%+ is seasonal; make every visit resolve a gifting problem.

### Key choices
- Layout: urgency strip → occasion tabs (Birthday/Christmas/New baby/...) with switchable story panels
- Typography: Nunito + Fredoka; dense, scannable, ecommerce-grade
- Color: warm paper + confident red-orange, high-contrast CTAs
- Interaction: occasion tabs switch featured story; honest comparison table vs Wonderbly/Hooray Heroes; sibling bundle + subscription

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Q4 seasonality, SEO comparison traffic, AOV via bundles/subscription
- Weak at: emotional storytelling depth; busiest of the three

### Best for
Google search traffic ("personalized gift for 5 year old"), Christmas season, price-comparers.
