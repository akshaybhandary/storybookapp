Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2025

The short version: you upload a photo so we can put your child in a storybook. We use it to make your book, we don't sell it, we don't share it with advertisers, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

1. What we collect

  • The photo you upload — used to create the illustrations in your book.
  • The details you type in — your child's first name, age, and the story idea you describe.
  • Your email address — only if you contact us, join our list, or place an order that needs delivery or a receipt.
  • Order details — shipping address and payment confirmation if you buy a printed or digital book. Payments are processed by our payment provider; we never see or store your card number.

2. Children's photos — how they're handled

LumiFables is made for parents and guardians. The service is directed at adults, and we rely on your consent for everything we do with your child's photo:

  • You must be the parent or legal guardian of the child in the photo (we ask you to confirm this before generating a book).
  • The photo is processed by AI to produce stylized illustrations. We use Google's Gemini AI services for this, which means the photo is transmitted securely to Google for processing.
  • We do not use photos to train advertising profiles, we do not sell them, and we do not publish them anywhere.
  • Photos are retained only as needed to create your book and let you re-download it, and can be deleted on request (see below).

3. Where books live until you save them

Stories you create are stored in your browser's local storage on your device, so your library stays private to you. Books are only uploaded to our servers when needed to generate illustrations or fulfil an order.

4. Who else is involved

  • Google (Gemini) — generates story text and illustrations from your photo and inputs.
  • Netlify — hosts our website.
  • Our payment provider — handles checkout for paid books.
  • Our print partner — prints and ships physical books (name and details are shown at checkout when you order).

Each provider only receives what they need to do their job, and we choose providers that offer strong data protection commitments.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Delete your photo, your book, or everything we hold about you.
  • Send you a copy of data associated with your account or order.
  • Stop sending you emails (every email has an unsubscribe link).

Email hello@lumifables.com and we'll take care of it, normally within 7 days.

6. Cookies & analytics

We use only the cookies strictly needed to run the site. If we add analytics, it will be privacy-preserving (no cross-site tracking, no ad profiles).

7. Changes

If we change this policy materially, we'll show the update on this page with a new date.

Questions? hello@lumifables.com
